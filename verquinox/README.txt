MATTBEAR – VERQUINOX — ASSET REBUILD

START
1. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. If folder loading is blocked, double-click
   start-verquinox.bat and open the local address shown.
2. Choose a folder containing audio samples, or press Try demo sounds.
3. Wait for local analysis, then press Generate mix.
4. Press Play. Lock lanes you like and reroll the others.
5. Press Export mix WAV when the combination is ready.

SOUND DESIGN
- Six distinct master-FX presets plus Random Wild.
- Filter, drive, bit crush, tempo-linked delay, reverb, tremolo, and compression.
- Effects remain live while playing and are printed into exported WAV files.

Your audio is decoded and analyzed in the browser. It is not uploaded.

SIMPLE MODE
- Folder: imports supported files from one folder and its subfolders.
- Generate mix: chooses six compatible samples.
- Lock: keeps a lane when generating another mix.
- Reroll: replaces only one lane.
- Solo / Mute: inspect a lane or remove it from the mix.
- Panic: immediately clears all active sounds.
- Export: downloads one loop-length stereo WAV.

ADVANCED MODE
- Match priorities adjust how choices are scored.
- Bars sets the rendered loop length.
- Each lane gains a transpose control and analysis details.

CURRENT LIMITS
- Tempo and key detection are estimates. Short one-shots may be labeled imperfectly.
- v0.1 synchronizes samples with playback-rate matching. Independent time stretching
  and pitch shifting are planned for the native desktop engine.
- Browser codec support varies. WAV and MP3 are the safest choices.

This edition uses the supplied individual MATTBEAR sampler graphics as real
controls and scenery. It does not need an internet connection.
