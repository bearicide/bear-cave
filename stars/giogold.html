LABEL DECODER

What does % Daily Value mean? 🔎

The %DV helps show how much a nutrient in one serving contributes to a general daily diet. It does not tell you whether you personally need a supplement.

SUPPLEMENT FACTS · EXAMPLE ONLYServing size: 1 tablet
Vitamin C90 mg · 100% DV
Vitamin D20 mcg · 100% DV
Magnesium42 mg · 10% DV

Illustrative values based on common U.S. adult Daily Values, not a product label or a personal dosage recommendation. Ask a clinician or pharmacist about interactions and individual needs.

Five questions before buying ＋
What is the serving size? What amount am I actually getting? Is the dose appropriate for me? Does it interact with anything I take? Can I verify the manufacturer and any testing claims?

Vitamin & Mineral Basics

Learn what common nutrients do, where they appear in food, and why individual needs vary.

Open the guide ＋
Vitamins and minerals help the body perform normal functions, but more is not automatically better. Vitamin D supports calcium regulation; B12 is involved in nerve function and blood-cell production; vitamin C supports connective tissue; magnesium contributes to muscle and nerve function; zinc supports immune function. Food sources and individual needs vary.
