CREATE TOGETHER

The Idea Exchange

Start a conversation, pitch a segment, share a project or find a creative collaborator.

Open the guide ＋
Pitch a segment in four parts: (1) the idea in one sentence, (2) who should participate, (3) what viewers get from it, and (4) the ideal length. Try a 15-minute creator spotlight, a themed open-mic, or a two-host Q&A. Before going live, agree on introductions, speaking order, and who handles chat.
KNOW BEFORE YOU JOIN

BIGO Agency Guide

Learn what to ask agencies about onboarding, contracts, targets, payouts, support and exit terms before joining.

Explore this topic ＋
Ask for written agency terms covering required hours, payouts, support, exclusivity and exit rules. Verify through official channels; never share passwords.
