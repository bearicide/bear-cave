    • Freesound — Find one-shots, textures and loops. Filter for CC0 or check attribution and commercial-use terms on each sound.

    • Looperman — Browse loops by BPM, key and genre. Read the terms and distinguish loops from acapellas.

    Turntable help

    Drag the record or waveform to scratch. Drag a number to adjust it, or double-tap it to type.

    The mixer estimates BPM for imported tracks. Auto-sync matches their speed when both decks have a BPM. Set a cue on the first beat of each track to help line them up.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    A / L play or pause each deck. Q / P jump to its cue. Space starts both decks when stopped. Press Space again, or press Esc, to stop everything. Arrow keys adjust a selected number box.
