Find free tracks and samples
  • Freesound — Find one-shots, textures and loops. Filter for CC0 or check attribution and commercial-use terms on each sound.
  • Pixabay Music — Find full backing tracks. Check the license summary before sharing recordings or redistributing audio.
  • Looperman — Browse loops by BPM, key and genre. Read the terms and distinguish loops from acapellas.
Keyboard shortcuts

A / L play or pause each deck. Q / P jump to its cue. Escape stops both decks. Sliders also work with arrow keys.
