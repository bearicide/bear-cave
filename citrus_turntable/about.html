Citrus Turntable

About

Citrus Turntable is a browser DJ mixer by MATTBEAR and Bearicide Productions. Play two tracks, blend them with the crossfader, and record the mix.

Tracks and tools

The mixer comes with ten licensed funk, disco, lounge, house, hip hop, soul, and trance excerpts. Load your own audio from your device or add music from a link to the audio file. Use the waveforms to scratch and the deck controls to set cues, loop tracks, or match tempo.

Files you choose from your device are played in the browser.

Sample credits

Starter tracks are by Kevin MacLeod, 1000 Handz featuring Ketsa, and Ketsa, edited into short excerpts and licensed under CC BY 4.0.
