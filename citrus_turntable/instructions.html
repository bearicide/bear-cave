Citrus Turntable

Instructions

The two decks already have tracks loaded. Press Play on either deck, or Play All in the mixer.

  1. Load a track. Use Add music from your device in the Crate to preview your files and see their waveforms. Choose Load A or Load B to send a track to a deck, or drop a file directly on a deck. If you found a song online, download the file first and add it from your device. If you already have a link to the audio file itself, use Add from a link.
  2. Mix. Play both decks. Drag left or right on the XY pad to blend the decks. Move up or down to change the master volume. Drag the Bass, Mids, Treble, and Level numbers to shape the sound.
  3. Match tempo. Auto-sync uses the BPM values shown on the decks. For your own tracks, enter the BPM and set a cue on the first beat. Use Sync now to match tempo manually.
  4. Scratch or skip. Drag the waveform forward or backward with a mouse or finger. Drag the Playhead number to skip. Double-tap or double-click any number box to type a value, or press Enter when the box has focus.
  5. Save a mix. Press Record, then Finish recording. Use the Download mix link to save the result.

Keyboard shortcuts

A and L play or pause the decks. Q and P jump to their cues. Space starts both decks. Esc stops both.
