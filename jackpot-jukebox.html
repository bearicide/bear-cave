HOW IT WORKS + REEL GUIDE

THE FIVE REELS

DRUMS
Chooses the beat, fills and break pattern.
BASS
Chooses the bass instrument and riff: sub, wobble, Reese, rumble or glide.
CHORDS
Chooses the harmonic texture. Every option remains in D minor.
HOOK
Chooses the lead phrase: chops, riffs, arcade tones, shouts or sparkles.
FX
Adds a siren, jackpot phrase, riser, reverse swell or impact.

CONTROLS

  • Hold: keeps that reel during the next spin.
  • Auto Spin: keeps generating mixes until switched off.
  • Heat: rises with matches and adds extra percussion and fills.
  • Queued: the new result is waiting for the next bar so the music stays smooth.
  • Record Jam: captures the performance at a consistent mastered level and downloads it when stopped.
  • Normalization: compression and final limiting keep quiet and busy combinations close in loudness without clipping.
  • Flashes: disables animated light effects without muting the music.
