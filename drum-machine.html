Build the beat.

Program a 16-step pattern, tap the live pads, record hits into the sequence, change kits, add swing, and control it from MIDI. Everything is generated locally in the browser.

Tip: click steps to program. Turn on REC and tap pads while the sequencer plays to write hits into the nearest step.
Quick start
  1. Choose a kit and tempo.
  2. Choose a paint brush: 1× hit, 2× split, 4× split, or erase. Click-drag across the grid to paint.
  3. Press Play to hear the loop.
  4. Use the pads live with mouse, touch, keyboard, or MIDI notes 36–43.
  5. Turn on REC while playing to capture pad hits.
  6. Save patterns in your browser or export them as JSON.
