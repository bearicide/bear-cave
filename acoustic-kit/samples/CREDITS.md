# Acoustic recordings
Salamander Drumkit by Alexander Holm (Rytmenpinne).
Source: https://github.com/studiorack/salamander-drumkit
Original source: https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-drumkit/
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported. See LICENSE.txt.
These selected recordings were transcoded from FLAC to 160 kbps MP3 by MATTBEAR; no synthesized replacement hits. The additional middle tom reuses the recorded 12-inch rack tom played three semitones lower at runtime. Original rack and floor tom recordings are 12x7 and 14x14 inches; kick is 18x18, snare 14x5. Two alternate takes per available velocity layer are included.
