      How to play

      Press A S W D, or click or tap the matching pads when the notes cross the hit line. Hit stacked notes together. Use Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate a focused button. M toggles sound. Space on the playfield or Esc pauses or resumes.

      Closer hits earn more points. Build a streak to raise your multiplier. Missing a note or tapping off beat breaks the streak.

      ENTER Play / replaySPACE Pause / resumeM Toggle sound

      Beast adds rapid fills and two-pad chords with tighter timing. Hit both pads together on a chord.

      In HUNT mode, land 12 Perfect or Great hits to charge. Press E or tap HUNT to double hit points for 8 beats. Misses still break combos.

      Earn one Claw every 8 Perfect hits in HUNT mode, up to 3 per run. Press R or tap Reverse within 2 seconds of a miss, before your next judgment, to restore your combo. The miss still counts toward accuracy.

      Best scores are saved on this device for each track and difficulty.
